Project page layouts

Three ways to organise a case study, drawn as rough sketches in your style. Swipe each row sideways: every box is one "screen" as you move down the page. The grey boxes are text, the green and blue boxes are screenshots.

CTap-to-open blobs

The most playful: one big screenshot, and small outlined blobs around it. Tap one (Problem, Process, Result) and it opens with the details. Very personal, but a skimming visitor can miss things hidden inside.

How I'd choose

B is the safest: it shows your work clearly and is quick to skim, and it keeps your signature look through the numbers, blobs and outlines. If you want it to feel more like your home screen, I can borrow one idea from A: each chapter fills the screen and snaps into place as you scroll. C is memorable but risky for a hiring manager with two minutes.
