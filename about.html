Hi, I’m Nardos

I am a product designer based in Addis Ababa. I design apps and the structure underneath them, from the first question to the last screen, with real care for the people who use them every day.

I am open to roles and freelance, remotely or in person.

Away from screens I make leather goods by hand. I plan each piece the way I plan an app: I work out the flat cut dimensions, how the cards sit and whether a banknote clears, and then I make it myself. It keeps me honest about materials, edges and what really holds up in daily use.
